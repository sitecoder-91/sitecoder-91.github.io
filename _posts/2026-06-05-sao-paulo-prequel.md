Hello, my loves.

Before the quiet shores of the UK, before the surf and the slacklines of my current life, there was Sampa. 

São Paulo. A sprawling, electric metropolis of twenty million souls, where the concrete rises like mountains and the night is always alive with a restless, throbbing pulse. It is the city of my birth, and the place where I first learned to translate the whispers of my own desire into a language of absolute freedom.

<img src="{{ '/assets/img/sao-paulo-mood.png' | relative_url }}" alt="São Paulo night mood" class="blog-post-image">

Many of you know me for my confidence, my playfulness, and perhaps my appreciation for the finer things in life. But every fire has a spark, and mine was lit in the hidden corners of Sampa’s nocturnal scene. 

It started nearly a decade ago. I’ve always had a kinky side—a curiosity that standard boundaries could never quite contain. So, when a former partner suggested we explore one of São Paulo’s exclusive swing venues, I didn't hesitate. I walked into that world of velvet, low lights, and soft music, and instead of feeling out of place, I felt an intoxicating sense of homecoming.

There is a unique power in watching others drop their masks. In those rooms, under the warm glow of city lights filtering through high windows, I realized that desire isn't something to be hidden. It’s an art. 

Soon, my curiosity blossomed into something much larger. I began to navigate the scene independently, building relationships with high-end establishments and exclusive bars that catered to international visitors. São Paulo is a global hub, and my ability to speak fluent, conversational English became my passport. I wasn't just offering companionship; I was offering a connection—an intellectual and sensual sanctuary for travellers far from home.

I loved the chess game of it. The thrill of holding a man's gaze across a dimly lit lounge, letting him think he was the one orchestrating the evening, while we both knew the current was carrying him exactly where I wanted him to go. 

I was enjoying so much the thrill of the hunt, the power of seduction, that I started hosting swingers parties at my place in [27 R. Santo Henrique](https://w3w.co/task.greeting.answer)... and I still do when I'm back home.

But as Sampa's concrete skyline grew familiar, I started to dream of wider horizons. The whispers of the girls who had traveled abroad, the allure of independent success on a global scale, and the simple desire to see how my Brazilian warmth would translate in cooler climates... it all pointed in one direction.

So, I packed my bags, took a deep breath, and set my sights on the UK.

It was a leap of faith, darling. And if you want to know what happened when I landed... well, you’ll just have to read about the spark that started it all in my next post.

Tell me, have you ever chased a desire that scared you just a little?
